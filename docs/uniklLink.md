# Guide to use UNIKL LINK apps

1. If you're experiencing problem logging in via "Sign in with Microsoft", please don't use as it has technical issues. Instead, key-in your email without "@s.unikl.edu.my", your ECITIE password, and click Login.

2. Ensure your app is the latest version : Please ensure you are using Version 2.3.5 (IOS) or 2.3.7 (Android). You can verify your version number at the bottom of the login screen. If you are not on the latest version, please update via the links below:

Android: Download Here: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=my.edu.unikl.uniklconnect
iOS: Download Here - https://online1.unikl.edu.my/apps_unikllink/beta/ios/ * please uninstall existing app first

3. If you're on ios and you see "Untrusted Enterprise Developer" message when opening the app, follow the steps below to trust the developer profile:

a. Unlock your iPhone or iPad and open the Settings app.
b. Tap General.
c. Tap VPN & Device Management (iOS 15 and later) or Profiles & Device Management (iOS 14 and earlier).
d. Under Enterprise App, tap Universiti Kuala Lumpur.
e. Tap Trust "Universiti Kuala Lumpur".
f. When prompted, tap Trust again to confirm.
g. Enter your device passcode if required.
h. For newer iOS versions, restart your device to complete the setup.
i. After restarting, return to your Home Screen and open UniKL Link normally.

4. Please also make sure you don't use dark theme.

5. If you still can't login, kindly confirm whether you can login into Student Portal using the same password.
